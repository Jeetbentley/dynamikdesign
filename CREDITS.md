# Image Credits

Every image on the site is listed here and in `src/data/images.ts` (the manifest the site reads).

**Status:** no images have been downloaded yet. Each slot currently renders a themed placeholder block.

## How to fill a slot

1. Search Unsplash (unsplash.com) or Pexels (pexels.com) with the search terms below. Free-license images only.
2. Avoid visible car or brand logos, close-up identifiable faces, and stock clichés (handshakes, people pointing at screens).
3. Save the file at the path shown, under `/public` (e.g. `public/images/home/hero.jpg`). JPG, about 2400 px wide for heroes and 1600 px for everything else.
4. Fill in Source, Photographer and License here **and** in `src/data/images.ts`.
5. Rebuild. The placeholder is replaced automatically once the file exists.
